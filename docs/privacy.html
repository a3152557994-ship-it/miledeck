Privacy Policy — MileDeck

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

MileDeck ("we", "our", or "the App") is engineered with a strict 100% offline-first, zero-tracking privacy architecture. Your driving routes, home and client addresses, vehicles, odometer readings, and IRS mileage logs belong exclusively to you.

1. Zero Cloud Data Collection

MileDeck does not require account registration and never transmits, uploads, or sells your GPS coordinates, trips, addresses, vehicles, or tax deduction summaries to any developer servers or third parties. All records are stored locally on your device.

2. Location & Motion Activity Usage

When you start a live drive or enable Automatic Drive Detection, MileDeck uses Apple's native CoreLocation and CoreMotion frameworks strictly on-device to measure trip distance and detect automotive starts/stops. Background location is accessed only when you explicitly grant permission and enable Automatic Drive Detection.

3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All purchases and subscriptions are processed securely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit 2). We never receive or store your payment or credit card credentials.

4. Analytics & Advertising

MileDeck contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero tracking pixels, and zero behavioral analytics.

5. Data Export & Deletion

You can export your complete mileage ledger anytime as an IRS Schedule C PDF, CSV spreadsheet, or local JSON backup from Reports and Settings. Deleting the app removes all locally stored records from your device.
