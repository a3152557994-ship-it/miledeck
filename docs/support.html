Frequently Asked Questions

How does MileDeck handle the 2026 IRS mid-year rate change?
MileDeck automatically inspects every trip's date: trips between Jan 1 – Jun 30, 2026 calculate at $0.725/mile, and trips from Jul 1 – Dec 31, 2026 calculate at $0.76/mile—and both periods are itemized separately on your IRS Schedule C PDF logbook.

How do I restore my Lifetime Pro purchase on a new iPhone?
Open MileDeck → Settings → tap "Restore Purchases" while signed into the same Apple Account.

How do I back up my mileage log before switching phones?
Go to Settings → tap "Export Local JSON Backup" to save your full drive history and vehicles to iCloud Drive, Files, or email, then tap "Restore from JSON Backup" on your new device.

Does MileDeck upload my GPS routes to any server?
Never. MileDeck runs 100% offline on your iPhone and iPad with zero account required.
