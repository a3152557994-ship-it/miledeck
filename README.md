# MileDeck (`com.yanghe.miledeck`)

100% offline native iOS / iPadOS Automatic Mileage & 2026 IRS Tax Deduction Tracker built with SwiftUI, CoreLocation, CoreMotion, PDFKit, and StoreKit 2.

- **Verified 2026 IRS Split-Period Rate Engine**: Automatically applies **$0.725/mi** (Jan 1 – Jun 30, 2026) and **$0.76/mi** (Jul 1 – Dec 31, 2026), plus Medical/Moving ($0.21/mi), Charity ($0.14/mi), custom per-mile / per-km overrides, and Miles/KM toggle.
- **Card-Swipe Drive Deck**: Swipe right for Business deduction, left for Personal, with purpose tags and 1-tap Undo.
- **Audit-Ready Schedule C PDF & CSV Reports**: Live embedded vector `PDFKit` logbook with H1/H2 split summaries and vehicle odometer records.
- **Honest One-Time Pricing**: Free tier includes unlimited tracking + 20 classified/exported drives per month; optional `$14.99` Lifetime unlock (`com.yanghe.miledeck.pro.lifetime`), `$9.99/yr` Annual (3-day free trial), or `$1.99/mo` Monthly.
